[00:03:15] Marcus: Most teams run pricing experiments on the number and never touch the packaging. That's
backwards. In our experience, changing what's included in a tier moved revenue far more than changing the
price of an existing tier ever did.

[00:09:40] Marcus: One pattern that held across almost every SaaS pricing test we ran: usage-based add-ons
outperform flat tier upgrades for expansion revenue, but only once a customer has already had a "wow"
moment with the core product. Introduce usage pricing too early and it just reads as nickel-and-diming.

[00:15:20] Marcus: We also found that a visible "most popular" badge on the middle tier increased
conversion to that tier by a meaningful margin — but it only works if the middle tier is genuinely the
best value for the majority of your users. If you fake it, support tickets and downgrade requests spike
within the first billing cycle.

[00:22:55] Marcus: The biggest trap in pricing experimentation is testing during a low-traffic period and
declaring a winner too early. We standardized on a minimum two full billing cycles before calling any
pricing test, because first-cycle behavior and renewal behavior are driven by pretty different psychology.
