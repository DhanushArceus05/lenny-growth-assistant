[00:02:10] Priya: The single biggest mistake I see product-led growth teams make is treating onboarding
as a tour instead of a task. A tour shows you buttons. A task gets you to your first real result. If a
new user hasn't experienced the core value of the product within their first session, you've already
lost most of the battle for retention.

[00:06:45] Priya: We rebuilt our onboarding around what I call the "one true action" — the single thing
that, if a user does it in session one, correlates most strongly with week-four retention. For us that
was inviting a teammate. Everything else in onboarding became scaffolding toward that one action, and we
ruthlessly cut every screen that didn't serve it.

[00:11:30] Priya: A concrete tactic: we moved account creation to *after* the user got a taste of the
product, not before. Signup-then-explore has a much higher drop-off than explore-then-signup for
horizontal tools, because you're asking for commitment before you've delivered any value.

[00:18:02] Priya: We also instrumented a "time to first value" metric explicitly, not just activation
rate. Activation rate can look healthy while time-to-value silently creeps up, and that's usually the
first sign your onboarding flow is accumulating friction nobody's noticed yet.
